     - `IndexingMemoryController` logs repeatedly indicate throttling due to the inability to keep up with segment writing. This is a direct consequence of the high indexing load.

   - **Logs:** The logs show frequent and lengthy garbage collection cycles, particularly the `old` GC. This is a common symptom of high memory pressure caused by the indexing load. The heap is constantly being compressed, impacting performance.

4. **Thread Pool Issues:**
   - **Logs:** The `ThreadPool` logs show the write thread pool is frequently overloaded, with queues full. This is a direct result of the high indexing load and the reindexing task.

The primary root cause is the **overloaded indexing load** driven by the reindexing job. The system is simply unable to handle the volume of data being written to the index.

2. **Indexing Load Management:**
   - **Throttle Indexing:** Implement more aggressive throttling on the indexing process to prevent it from overwhelming the system.
